Privacy Policy

Free live call with Max and Austin

1. Who this covers

This free live call is hosted jointly by two separate companies: Pured Media Studios LLC DBA Pured Media ("Pured Media"), Max's company, and Whitelist Wealth, Austin McCoo's company ("Whitelist Wealth"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you register for the call, visit this site, or get messages from us. Please read it. If you do not agree, please do not register.

Pured Media runs this page and holds your registration. Your information is collected and stored in Pured Media's customer system (GoHighLevel), which Max's company runs with help from its marketing agency. Pured Media sends the text messages about this call. It sends the emails about this call for both hosts. In this policy, "we" means Pured Media, unless it says Whitelist Wealth.

2. Information we collect

What you give us. When you register: your full name, email address, and phone number, and whether you checked the email box and the text box. We also keep a record of the wording you agreed to and when.

Where you came from. When you visit the page, we record where you came from, such as the site that sent you or the tag in the link you clicked (for example "instagram"). We hold this in your browser for that visit and save it with your registration, so we know which host's link brought you. It also tells us whose registration yours is (see section 4).

What our servers see. Basic visit information, such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you viewed, and the time. We use Vercel's analytics for this, to learn how the page is used.

Your calls and messages. If you reply to us, book a call, or speak with us, we keep a record of that.

The call itself. We may record the call, including video, audio, the chat, and questions you ask. If you join, ask a question, or type in the chat, your name, voice, image, and words may be in the recording. Zoom, which hosts the call, may also receive the name and email you use to join. Attendees' faces, names, and chat are left out of any replay we share. If you do not want to be recorded, keep your camera and microphone off and do not use the chat.

Cookies. The registration form is provided by GoHighLevel and may use cookies or similar tools so the form works. If you reached this page from an ad, the ad platform (for example Meta) may have its own information about your click, under its own policy. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

We do not ask for payment details to register. The call is free.

3. How we use it

  • To register you and send you the call details and the Zoom link.
  • To send reminders, and the replay or follow-up for the call.
  • To email you if you checked the email box, and to text you if you checked the text box (see sections 6 and 7).
  • To call you about your registration or about booking a one-on-one call, if you gave a phone number.
  • To answer your questions, and to keep records that show your consent.
  • To see which links and pages bring people to the call, and to improve the page and the call.
  • To protect our systems, follow the law, and handle any dispute.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Two hosts: whose information it is

If you came through Pured Media's link, Pured Media holds your registration and uses it for the purposes in section 3. We do not give it to Whitelist Wealth. If you checked the email box, you may get emails from Austin about this call. Pured Media sends them, so Whitelist Wealth does not receive your details through them. If you reply to a host or book with a host, that host receives what you send.

If you came through a link from Whitelist Wealth, your registration belongs to Whitelist Wealth. Pured Media keeps it in its system to run the call, and uses it only to send you messages about this call and to deal with a one-on-one call if you ask for one. Pured Media does not use it to send you promotions for its own programs unless Whitelist Wealth agrees. This holds even though the text box mentions updates about Pured Media programs. At a date Whitelist Wealth sets, Pured Media gives Whitelist Wealth the name and email address of the people who checked the email box and have not unsubscribed. Phone numbers and text opt-in records are not included. After that, if Whitelist Wealth asks, Pured Media deletes or stops using its copy for anyone who did not book with Pured Media. Whitelist Wealth handles its own contacts under its own practices.

Each host is responsible for how it uses the information it holds. Your text-message consent goes to Pured Media only, and does not let Whitelist Wealth text you. See section 6.
